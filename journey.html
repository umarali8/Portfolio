Phase 1 — Foundations

Weeks 1–2

Audited my own AI workflow before touching a model. Drew the sitemap for a portfolio that didn't exist yet. Ran the starter notebooks and framed my research question. Learned the prompt ladder — and then had to answer the hardest question of the whole program early: "What are you proving?"

Phase 2 — Identity & Data

Weeks 3–4

Built an identity kit so my work would look like mine, not a template. Curated images, mapped content to CTAs, wrote a full data contract for the model, and shipped a working automation workflow — plus my first pass at agent concepts and MCP basics.

Phase 3 — Building & Shipping

Weeks 5–6

Designed and built my own personal agent from scratch. Put my baseline model score to the test with a Top-10 review. My personal website went live on the FlyRank domain. Then came the scariest deliverable: "Survive the Crit" — real feedback, on real work, in real time.

Phase 4 — Launch

Weeks 7–8

Planted my flag — domain live, badge earned. Wrote the content action playbook. Validated every research claim before shipping the paper. And the final week: documenting it all, recording the demo, and telling the story on this page.

The numbers behind the 8 weeks

🏆 Ranked 81 out of 6,600 participants
🎓 18 certificates earned along the way
📅 Attended every single live event — workshops, guest mentor sessions, crit days, all of it
📈 LinkedIn profile audit score: 82/100 — up 44 points from where I started

None of it would mean much sitting in a private notebook, so it's all public — the model, the paper, the agent, the site you're reading this on.

To everyone who showed up to a session, left a comment on a rough draft, or pushed back on something I shipped too early — thank you. This one's for you.

If you're a few weeks into a program like this and it feels like too many small tasks to add up to anything: they add up. Keep going.
